// The people behind it

Founders

URL2AUDIO was built out of a real need — fast, professional-grade audio processing without the middleman.

$tey Muletti
Founder & Artist

URL2AUDIO is my entire release workflow — from ripping reference tracks and detecting keys, to separating stems and opening everything straight in FL Studio. Every single release on my SoundCloud is produced, processed, and delivered completely in-house. No labels, no middlemen, no outsourcing. Just the tool, the DAW, and the music. I built this because I needed it, and now I ship it so other independent artists can move just as fast.

SoundCloud
Patrick Garcia
Developer & Drummer

Patrick is the engineer who made it all run. A full-stack developer and lifelong drummer, he architected the stem separation pipeline, built the desktop application framework from the ground up, and handled the system tuning and cross-platform compatibility that makes it work on Windows, macOS, and Linux without missing a beat. This project sits right at the crossroads of his two worlds, and he had a great time making them collide. Building something that actually helps artists make music faster is the kind of work that doesn't feel like work.
